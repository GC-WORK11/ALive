04 · the hard parts

What made Delhi hard

Three problems decided whether this was a toy or a product.

A grid is not a street
Satellite cells are ~1 km². We interpolate stations + model onto street-level hexes so the map reads per-road, not per-district.
Feeds die mid-demo
Station APIs drop keys and go dark. The app degrades honestly — satellite mode, labelled — instead of freezing or faking.
Privacy vs. proof
Dose needs your track; tracking people is creepy. So the GPS trail stays on the phone — only anonymous counters leave.

Built on open data — OSM, OpenFreeMap, Open-Meteo/CAMS, CPCB/WAQI/DPCC, TomTom — and a C++ engine that answers in milliseconds, not seconds.
